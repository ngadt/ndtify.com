The client

LITHACO designs, installs and maintains solar power, battery storage and EV charging systems across Vietnam, from family rooftops to 5 MWp factory roofs. Their buyers range from homeowners to factory managers and public institutions, and each of them asks a different first question.

The site needed to answer the most important one straight away, “how much will I save?”, then route each type of buyer to the right solution and turn that interest into a site-survey request.

What we built

01

Electricity-bill calculator in the hero

Visitors enter their average monthly bill (or tap a preset of 2, 4, 8 or 20 million VND) and the calculator returns the recommended kWp, estimated savings per year and years to payback, using the engineering team’s own formula. No phone number is required to see the estimate, which removes the biggest point of friction before a lead is ready.

02

Solutions by buyer type

Separate paths for households, commercial buildings, factories, schools and hospitals, and government agencies, each with the arguments and system sizes that matter to that audience.

03

Cost & ROI estimator with ready-made packages

A detailed estimate page maps the bill to a prebuilt package and says so honestly when no package fits, pushing those visitors to a custom quote instead of a dead end.

04

Proof, front and centre

Seven manufacturers that authorise LITHACO as a distributor, a nationwide map of operating systems, 2025 awards, ISO certifications and TV/press coverage, all placed where a cautious B2B buyer looks for reassurance.

05

Content that answers real buyer questions

A five-step process from phone consultation to maintenance, plus articles such as whether old metal roofs can take solar panels, LFP vs NMC batteries, and which system suits a 5-million-VND bill.
