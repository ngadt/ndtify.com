The client

Kim Hoe & Co has fitted tyres in Singapore since 1974 and stocks the big names: Michelin, Pirelli, Dunlop, Goodyear, Continental, Yokohama, Federal and more. The workshop carries thousands of tyres, plus car batteries and rims.

The goal was to let drivers find the exact tyre for their car online and buy it before they arrive, turning a phone-and-walk-in business into one that also sells around the clock.

What we built

01

Tyre-size filter on the homepage

Width, height (profile), rim diameter and brand selectors narrow thousands of SKUs down to the tyres that fit, without making the shopper dig through category pages.

02

Shop by vehicle

Drivers who don’t know their tyre size can choose their vehicle instead.

03

Tyres, batteries and rims by brand

Brand catalogues for each product line, with warranty information such as the Pirelli TYRElife programme.

04

Checkout and marketing that are already wired up

WooCommerce checkout with PayPal, Klaviyo for email marketing, and WhatsApp chat for the many customers in Singapore who would rather message than call.

05

Trust signals for a 50-year-old business

Recent-purchase notifications, awards, testimonials, “how to buy” and FAQ pages, and the workshop’s story, all supporting a premium but friendly brand.
