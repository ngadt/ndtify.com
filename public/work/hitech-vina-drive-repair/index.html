The client

Hitech Vina has repaired variable frequency drives and other industrial electronics since 2008, both at its Ho Chi Minh City workshop and on site at customers’ factories. It works on ABB, Siemens, KEB, Danfoss, Schneider, SEW, Lenze, Vacon and more.

Its customers are in a hurry, because every hour a machine is down costs money. The site therefore gets out of the way: identify the drive, see that this workshop fixes it, and get in touch in one tap.

What we built

01

Brand-first navigation

A “which brand is your drive?” selector lists each manufacturer with its common series (ABB ACS150/ACS355, Siemens Sinamics G120/S120, KEB COMBIVERT F5/F6, Schneider Altivar), because that is how engineers think about their equipment.

02

Search by nameplate code

The header search takes the code printed on the drive (for example ACS580 or 6SE…) and goes straight to the relevant repair page.

03

One contact for the whole control cabinet

Service pages for servo drives, PLCs and I/O modules, HMIs, soft starters, DC drives, compressors, flow meters and scheduled maintenance, so one search can turn into a long-term customer.

04

Transparent process

Receive, diagnose, quote, repair and load-test, hand over: five steps with the promise up front of a quote before any work starts and a load test before anything leaves the workshop.

05

Case studies that build authority

Write-ups of real repairs (Siemens SIMOREG DC converters, MASTERDRIVES, capacitor reforming) and location pages such as Bà Rịa–Vũng Tàu. These help the site rank for the long, specific searches that bring in qualified jobs.
