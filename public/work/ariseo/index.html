The client

ARISEO is a “hybrid curated multi-label fashion space” with physical stores at NorthStar, Ang Mo Kio in Singapore and on Itaewon-ro in Seoul. It stocks its own collections alongside labels such as BunnyHill Concept and DirtyCoins.

Each market needs its own storefront: a different language, currency, free-delivery threshold, legal footer and campaign imagery. We built and maintain two Shopify stores, ariseo.com for Singapore and ariseo.co.kr for Korea, from one shared theme codebase so the brand stays consistent while each store can run its own campaigns.

What we built

01

Two stores, one theme codebase

Both storefronts run a custom theme built on Shopify’s Dawn 15, managed in Git with a branch per store. Shared fixes ship to both, while each store keeps its own settings, campaigns and content.

02

Editorial, image-first design

Full-screen campaign slides (such as “ARISE : HELIX” and “AWAKEN”), a minimal serif wordmark and lots of white space let the photography sell the clothes.

03

Ship-to country and language switching

Shoppers choose where they are shipping to (Singapore, South Korea, Japan, China or the rest of the world) and can switch language, so prices, delivery and content match their market.

04

Built for Korean e-commerce rules

The Korean store is fully localised and shows the business registration and mail-order licence details that Korean online retailers are required to display.

05

Retail and online working together

Store locations in both cities, try-on information, free-delivery banners per market (S$120 in Singapore, ₩50,000 in Korea) and collections such as New In, Best Sellers and brand edits.
