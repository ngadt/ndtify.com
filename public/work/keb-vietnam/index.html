The client

KEB Vietnam supplies, replaces and repairs KEB COMBIVERT drives made by KEB Automation in Germany. These drives run injection-moulding machines, plastic extruders, textile lines and woodworking equipment all over Vietnam.

The typical visitor is a maintenance engineer standing next to a stopped machine. They don’t browse a catalogue. They have a model code on a sticker and an error on a display, and they need an answer fast. So the site is built around tools rather than brochure pages.

What we built

01

Nameplate decoder

Type a code such as 14.F5.A1E-3A0A and the site breaks it into its parts (power size, series, control type, equipment, housing and option) and links to the matching model page.

02

Search that forgives the way people type

The lookup accepts codes with or without dots and spaces (07F5B3A390A, 07 F5 B3A 390A) and searches more than 4,400 KEB F4, F5, F6 and S6 model codes.

03

Error-code library

Dedicated pages for common faults such as F6 error 44 (invalid power unit data) and E.OH (power module overheating), with quick checks an engineer can do before calling. These pages match exactly what people search for when a line goes down.

04

Pages for the machines drives live in

Content for OEM equipment such as Haitian injection-moulding machines, Battenfeld-Cincinnati extruders, Rieter textile machines and Homag woodworking lines, so engineers find the site by the machine name too.

05

One tap to a human

A “send a photo of the nameplate” flow plus floating phone, Zalo, Telegram and WhatsApp buttons, and a clear four-step repair process from diagnosis to warranty.
