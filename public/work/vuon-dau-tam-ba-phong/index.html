The client

Vườn Dâu Tằm Ba Phong is a 5-hectare organic mulberry farm in Tây Ninh, southern Vietnam. Entry is free: visitors pick ripe mulberries straight from the trees, relax under the coconut palms and take home the farm’s own products.

The website has two jobs. It has to convince families to make the drive at the weekend, and it has to sell the farm’s products to people who can’t visit. The design leans on real photography and a relaxed, hand-lettered tone to match the place.

What we built

01

“Today at the farm” live panel

A status card in the hero shows what is happening right now: whether berries are ripe for picking, opening hours, and parking for 45-seat coaches. Its greeting changes with the time of day, and after dark it invites visitors to book for tomorrow instead.

02

Free visit booking

“Book a free visit” is the primary call to action on every screen, so the farm knows who is coming and can prepare.

03

WooCommerce shop for farm products

Fresh mulberries, mulberry syrup, mulberry wine and dried mulberries, sold online with product pages that tell the story of how they are grown.

04

Story, gallery and press

The farm’s story, a photo corner for visitors, news from farm activities (sunrise yoga, tourism fairs) and press and TV coverage, including a feature on VTV9.

05

Chat that feels personal

A chat bubble that introduces the real people who answer, which suits a family-run farm far better than an anonymous contact form.
