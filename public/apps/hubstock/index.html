How it works

Add a second store in about a minute

  1. 01

    Import your catalog

    Install HubStock on the store that owns your stock. It becomes the master.

  2. 02

    Connect a store

    Install from the same browser and the stores link themselves, or hand a pairing code to whoever runs the other store. No API keys, no CSV files.

  3. 03

    Map & sync

    Map locations, match existing products by SKU and pick which fields sync. From then on stock and orders flow automatically.

Features

Everything multi-store sellers need to stop overselling

Real-time inventory sync

Stock lives on one master store. Every connected store mirrors it automatically, so two storefronts never fight over the same number.

Orders deduct master stock

An order on any connected store deducts stock on the master at the milestone you choose (paid or fulfilled). Cancellations restore it.

Match by SKU, no duplicates

Already selling on the other store? HubStock matches products by SKU, handle or title and links them instead of creating copies. Uncertain matches wait for your review.

Per-field product sync

Choose exactly what travels to each store: title, images, price, compare-at price, SKU, barcode, tags, vendor and more. Set global defaults, then override per store.

Metafields & definitions

Product and category metafields sync along with their definitions, so structured data arrives intact.

Location mapping

Map master locations to each destination store’s locations so stock lands in the right place.

History, scan & repair

Every stock movement lands in a searchable History log, and Scan & Repair plus a Health view catch anything that has drifted.

Low-stock alerts

Get notified when inventory drops below your own threshold.

FAQ

HubStock questions

Which store holds the stock?

The master store. Destination stores mirror its inventory and are locked to it, so stock is only ever edited in one place.

Will HubStock duplicate products that already exist on my other store?

No. It matches products by SKU (then handle or title) and links them. Anything uncertain waits for your review, and you can link in bulk or one product at a time with variant control.

What happens when an order is cancelled on a connected store?

The stock that was deducted from the master is restored automatically, and the movement is recorded in the History log.

Do destination stores pay too?

No. Destination stores use the Free plan; they are covered by the master store’s plan.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every paid plan includes a 15-day free trial.
